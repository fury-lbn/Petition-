Why are we proposing this change?

We spend over 35 hours weekly at school. The current uniform synthetic polyester fabric causes excessive heat, perspiration, and skin irritation while restricting movement during daily routine activities. Additionally, canteen choices are limited, and we hope to expand them.

We deeply respect Al-Nuzhum School and its leadership, presenting this proposal in a spirit of constructive cooperation.

What We Propose

  • Breathable Cotton Fabric

    Natural fabric that breathes and suits our climate.

  • Flexible Fit

    Practical ergonomic design allowing free movement throughout the day.

  • Balanced Colors

    Consistent and neat overall visual appearance.

  • Diverse Canteen Menu

    Broader food options including healthier choices.
